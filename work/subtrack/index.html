Finance & invoice managerMERN StackProject overview

Less admin. More clarity.

A dashboard for subscriptions, expenses, invoices, CRUD operations and backend integrations.

The idea

Keeping track of recurring subscriptions, everyday expenses and invoices can mean switching between several tools. SubTrack brings those workflows into a single dashboard.

The approach

Built around the MERN stack, the project connects a clear frontend to backend integrations and CRUD operations. The interface groups subscriptions, expenses and invoices into focused, understandable views.
